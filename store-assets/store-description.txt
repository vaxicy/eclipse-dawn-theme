Eclipse Dawn Theme quiets the browser down for long stretches of focus. A deep indigo window frame and tab strip sit above a charcoal-blue toolbar, while the new tab page drops to a near-black eclipse navy that lets your work stand out. A single periwinkle accent carries links, the active tab mark, and the store artwork. Every surface is a flat, single solid color, and each text tone is tuned to stay readable on its own layer. The palette lives entirely in the theme definition, so installing it simply recolors the browser.
